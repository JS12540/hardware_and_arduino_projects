Why GND goes on the right side of the channel

A four-legged button has two pairs of legs that are joined inside. Depending on which way it is turned, the joined legs are either the two on the same side of the channel, or the two in the same row. From a photo I can't tell which way yours is turned.

If pin 2 and GND both landed on the left side and the joined legs are on the same side, pin 2 would be tied to GND all the time and the LED would stay on. Putting pin 2 on the left and GND on the right works in either orientation.

Build order

  1. Button across the channel: legs in rows 41 and 43 on the left, and rows 41 and 43 on the right.
  2. Resistor: one leg in row 45, the other in row 51, left side.
  3. LED: long leg in row 51, short leg in row 54, left side. Row 51 is shared with the resistor, so that joins them.
  4. Orange wire: pin 2 to row 41, left side.
  5. Red wire: pin 8 to row 45, left side.
  6. Brown wire: GND to row 43, right side of the channel.
  7. Short brown jumper: row 43 on the right to row 54 on the left.
  8. Plug in USB and press the button. The LED should light only while it is held.
